# Subcommittee · Legal & Compliance

## Charter
Courier safety and labor exposure, partnership terms (iFood), consumer refunds and payment compliance.

## Convene when
Cash, courier-facing changes, BYOC/partnership questions, refunds and penalties that touch consumers or merchants.

## The test this subcommittee applies
What exposure does this create? What can we enforce? Is it disclosed? (General rules only; no invented legal citations.)

## Working rules
- The Chair names a lead for the subcommittee at the start of its turn block.
- At least two skeptic attacks per proposal; if the subcommittee has fewer skeptics, the Chair borrows from another subcommittee (usually the Numbers Auditor, Camila Ruiz or Iván Estrada).
- Findings go to the session folder; anything durable is promoted to `knowledge/` at close.
