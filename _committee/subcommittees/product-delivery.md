# Subcommittee · Product & Delivery

## Charter
Case 2 · Product management: roadmap across DeliveryX, Money and Platform pods, capacity, launch process, rollout readiness, Cash vs iFood BYOC.

## Convene when
Roadmap and capacity questions, launch process, rollout plans, anything that touches couriers or support.

## The test this subcommittee applies
Is there a metric, a guardrail and a rollback? Are hours and dates realistic? Is support ready?

## Working rules
- The Chair names a lead for the subcommittee at the start of its turn block.
- At least two skeptic attacks per proposal; if the subcommittee has fewer skeptics, the Chair borrows from another subcommittee (usually the Numbers Auditor, Camila Ruiz or Iván Estrada).
- Findings go to the session folder; anything durable is promoted to `knowledge/` at close.
