# Subcommittee · Merchant Operations

## Charter
Case 1 · Zero defects: diagnosis, Chi-chi-chicken and SuperCoffee action plans, merchant communication, phase 2 chains.

## Convene when
Any discussion of store-level actions, merchant incentives or penalties, the AM/POC communication plan, or whether a fix survives staff turnover.

## The test this subcommittee applies
Is the action owned by someone in the store? Does it survive turnover? Would the merchant actually do it?

## Working rules
- The Chair names a lead for the subcommittee at the start of its turn block.
- At least two skeptic attacks per proposal; if the subcommittee has fewer skeptics, the Chair borrows from another subcommittee (usually the Numbers Auditor, Camila Ruiz or Iván Estrada).
- Findings go to the session folder; anything durable is promoted to `knowledge/` at close.
