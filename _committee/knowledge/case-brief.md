# Case brief (paraphrased summary)

The original brief is confidential and is not stored here. This is a paraphrase of what the committee needs to know.

## Format
- Two cases, 25 minutes each.
- Use of AI is encouraged and evaluated: document what was asked, what came back, and how judgment was applied. The proposal must be the candidate's own thinking.

## Case 1 · Zero defects
- A portfolio of four parent chains (SuperCoffee, Chi-chi-chicken, RegioBurgers, The Mex Food) must reach a **1.5% inaccurate order rate in six months**.
- Only three issue types count: Missing Items, Partial Items in Order, Entire Order is Wrong.
- Commercial context per chain: engagement level, staff turnover, average monthly sales attainment (SuperCoffee high / mid / 120%; Chi-chi low / high / 78%; RegioBurgers high / low / 95%; The Mex Food mid / mid / 100%).
- Questions:
  1. Current inaccurate rate, by brand, and the key tasks performed.
  2. Detailed action plan for Chi-chi-chicken and SuperCoffee: pain points from user feedback, merchant-side and Uber-side actions, low-hanging fruit vs long-term, and a **weekly/monthly way of working** with the candidate's activities.
  3. How to communicate both plans to people less immersed (account manager and brand POC).

## Case 2 · Product management
- December; six-month roadmap with seven launches across three pods (DeliveryX, Money, Platform), each with roll-out month, scope, expected monthly trips and champion hours.
- Assumptions given: candidate is the DeliveryX champion; Money and Platform champions are peers; one Merchant Ops and one Earner Ops per country; one CommOps POC (20h); BAU of 16h per month each for billing (Money) and onboarding (Platform); one minor release per pod per month (10h champion, 2h CommOps).
- **Not given:** monthly capacity per champion (see `assumptions.md`).
- Questions:
  1. Roadmap for the three pods, opportunities and concerns.
  2. Key stakeholders and how to keep them engaged.
  3. General approach (structure, forums).
  4. What else to plan for a successful rollout.
  5. Choose between iFood BYOC and Cash for DeliveryX; how to talk to the deprioritized countries.
  6. Other major challenges of the role beyond rollouts and stakeholder management.
